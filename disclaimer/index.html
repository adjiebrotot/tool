Notes for particular tools

Money, tax and property
Borrowing, tax, retirement, loan, rent or buy, cost of living and pricing tools show estimates under the assumptions stated on each page. The loan types guide is educational: its examples are illustrations, not offers or advice, and the markets named beside each loan structure are where it is commonly sold, not a statement of local law. Cost-of-living and price figures are averages compiled from public sources and may not match your city, your lifestyle or today's prices.
Engineering
The Load Flow Simulator, Economic Dispatch and Unit Commitment Simulator, Capacity Expansion Planner and WEM Constraint Checker give indicative results from simplified models and the data they hold. Check them with a validated study tool, market or planning model, your own data and qualified engineering judgement (and financial advice for an investment) before any design, operating, safety, trading, investment, procurement or policy decision. Code from the PowerFactory Scripter and its samples must be reviewed and tested, ideally on a copy of your project, before any production or engineering use.
Market data
The DCA simulators fetch price history from Yahoo Finance, with Stooq as a fallback, through a small proxy I run on Cloudflare. That data may be delayed, incomplete, adjusted or inaccurate, and the service may be limited or stop. Ticker symbols, names and product availability in the ticker reference come from public information and may be outdated, so always check a ticker directly on Yahoo Finance before you use it.
Other tools
Exchange rates, maps, time zones and daylight saving rules come from public sources and may be late or wrong. The Random Picker is for fun and is not certified for prize draws, lotteries or anything legally binding.
