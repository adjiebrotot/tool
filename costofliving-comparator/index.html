What this assumes

    Questions

    Where do the city prices behind the estimates come from?

    They come from a crowd-sourced survey of 547 cities, bundled with the tool and updated on 22 May 2026, plus one set of exchange rates from the same date. Each city has separate indices for rent, groceries, eating out, electricity, water, gas, fuel and overall cost with and without housing, all rebased to Perth = 100. The base city cancels out of every ratio, so it never shows in a result.

    How is the salary I need in another city worked out?

    With the Savings ratio target, it is your expenses there divided by one minus your savings ratio today. If you save 25% now and the same lifestyle costs 3,000 a month there, you need 3,000 ÷ 0.75 = 4,000 net. With Nominal savings, it is the expenses there plus your current monthly savings converted at today's exchange rate.

    Why does the exchange rate not change my estimated living costs?

    Estimates use the rate the indices were priced at (May 2026), because each index is already a ratio of local prices. A weaker rupiah does not make a Perth lunch dearer in Australian dollars. Today's market rate, from a daily feed kept for six hours, only converts money that crosses the border: the home-currency figures, the nominal savings target, and the Remittance and International Travel rows.

    Does the salary it gives include income tax?

    No. Every salary is take-home pay, as you type it, and the model never works out tax or social charges. The salary you need is therefore a net figure, not a gross offer. To compare a gross offer, first turn it into your expected monthly net pay in that country, then enter that. Schooling and health cover paid through tax are also left out.

    How are Medical Care and Health Insurance rows scaled?

    Both rows use the World Bank's ICP 2021 health price level for the country, divided by its everyday price level, then multiplied by the city's cost-of-living index without housing. The ratio has no exchange rate in it, so it rides on the city's current index. Because it is set per country, two cities in one country share the same health ratio, and a country the ICP did not price shows no estimate.

    If I choose Exclude housing, is my rent taken out of my expenses?

    No. Exclude housing only switches the overall index to the one without rent, as when an employer pays for your home. Your Expenses figure is not changed. If it still contains rent, that rent is scaled by an index with no rent in it, which understates a move to a dearer city. Either leave the rent out of your figure or keep housing included.
