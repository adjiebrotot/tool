Privacy Policy

What happens to your data when you use Adjie Brotools.

← Other Tools

The short version

  • The figures, text and files you put into a tool are worked on in your own browser. They are not sent to me or stored on a server.
  • The site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies, to count visits. Some pages may also show ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies too.
  • A few tools fetch live data (maps, exchange rates, market prices) from other services. Those services see the request, as described below.
  • I do not sell your data, and there are no accounts, so I hold no profile of you. If you email me, I receive your email address and what you write.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What you type stays on your device

Every calculation, chart and conversion runs in your browser. Your inputs, and any file you open in a tool (a CSV, a video, a document), are not uploaded.

Remembered work

Most tools remember your last inputs and settings so they are still there next visit. Some also keep a copy of data they downloaded, such as price history or exchange rates, so they do not have to fetch it again. All of this is kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Saved scenarios

When you save a scenario, it is downloaded as a file to your device. Nothing is kept by the site.

Share links and embeds

A Share link or an embedded chart carries your inputs or chart data inside the link itself, after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the web server when the page is requested. Anyone you give the link to can read what it contains, so share it the way you would share the figures themselves.

An embedded chart shown on another website does not run Google Analytics, does not remember inputs and does not install a service worker. It still loads the site's files, fonts and chart libraries, so those hosts see the request, and the website showing the embed has its own privacy policy.

Offline use

When you visit, a service worker keeps copies of the site's pages, code, fonts and libraries in your browser so they load quickly and work offline, including if you install the site as an app. It stores site files, not your inputs.

What is collected when you visit

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details (page, browser, time) when your browser loads a page, and may keep them in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Analytics

I use Google Analytics to see which tools are used, how people find them and which pages need work. It records things such as the pages you view, how long you stay, the site that sent you, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or country). Google says Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. I only see totals and trends, not who you are. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Emails to me

If you email me, I receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. I use them only to reply and to fix what you report, and I do not add you to any list. The email is held by Google, as my email provider. Ask me and I will delete it.

Advertising

Some pages may show ads served by Google AdSense, which helps keep the tools free. Where ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.

If you opt out, you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown to them, as Google requires. See how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics
Cookies named _ga and _ga_… that tell one visit from a repeat visit. They last up to two years.
Google AdSense
Advertising cookies set by Google and its partners on pages that show ads, as described above.
Market data limit
A cookie named yf_req, set by the DCA simulators when you load prices, that counts how many price requests this browser has made today. It expires at the end of the day.
Remembered work
Local storage holding your last inputs and tool settings, panels you opened or closed, tours you have seen, and copies of downloaded price history and exchange rates. It stays on your device until you clear it.
Offline copies
The service worker's cache of site files, used to load pages quickly and offline.

Services some tools contact

Most pages load fonts from Google Fonts and many load code libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare) or jsDelivr. These services receive your IP address and browser details, as any website you load from does. A few tools also fetch live data:

Load Flow Simulator map
Map tiles from OpenStreetMap, EOX (satellite imagery) and Open Infrastructure Map when you use the map. Typing in the place search sends that text to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.
Exchange rates
The Cost of Living comparator, its map and the Rent vs Own sensitivity page fetch current exchange rates when they open, at most every few hours, from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) or the free currency API by fawazahmed0 (served through Cloudflare or jsDelivr). Only a request for the rates is sent, not your inputs.
DCA simulators
Price history is fetched through a small proxy I run on Cloudflare Workers, which asks Yahoo Finance, or Stooq as a fallback. The proxy receives the tickers and dates you load and your IP address; Yahoo Finance and Stooq see Cloudflare's request, not yours. To stop abuse the proxy keeps a count of requests per IP address for the day, which is deleted automatically within about 36 hours. Price responses are cached by Cloudflare for about 10 minutes by ticker, not by visitor, and the proxy keeps no record of who loaded which tickers. Cloudflare handles the request under its own privacy policy.
JSON Visualiser
If you load JSON from a web address, your browser fetches it directly from that address, so that site sees the request.

Where data goes

Google, GitHub, Cloudflare and the other services above run servers in many countries, including the United States, so information they receive may be handled outside the country you are in, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The tools keep working without them.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on to stop Analytics on every site.
  • Turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything it has stored on your device.

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Principles, the GDPR or a US state privacy law), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. As I hold no accounts or profiles, most such data sits with Google, GitHub or Cloudflare, but email me and I will help with anything I can reach. If you are not satisfied with my answer, you can complain to the privacy regulator where you live; in Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Children

The tools are not aimed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
