Privacy Policy

What happens to your data when you use Adjie Brotools.

← Other Tools

The short version

  • The figures, text and files you put into a tool are worked on in your own browser. They are not sent to me or stored on a server.
  • The site uses Google Analytics to count visits, and Google AdSense to show ads. Both use cookies.
  • A few tools fetch live data (maps, exchange rates, market prices) from other services. Those services see the request, as described below.
  • I do not sell your data, and there are no accounts, so I hold no profile of you.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who runs this site

Adjie Brotools (tool.adjiebrotots.com) is a personal project by Adjie Brotosukmono, based in Perth, Western Australia. In this policy, "I" and "me" mean Adjie. For any privacy question, email adjiebrotots@gmail.com.

What you type stays on your device

Every calculation, chart and conversion runs in your browser. Your inputs, and any file you open in a tool (a CSV, a video, a document), are not uploaded.

Remembered work

Most tools remember your last inputs so they are still there next visit. This is kept in your browser's local storage on your device only. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Saved scenarios

When you save a scenario, it is downloaded as a file to your device. Nothing is kept by the site.

Share links and embeds

A Share link or an embedded chart carries your inputs or chart data inside the link itself, after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the web server when the page is requested. Anyone you give the link to can read what it contains, so share it the way you would share the figures themselves.

Offline use

If you install the site as an app, or visit often, a service worker keeps copies of the pages and code in your browser so they load quickly and work offline. It stores site files, not your inputs.

What is collected when you visit

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details (page, browser, time) when your browser loads a page, and may keep them in its logs for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google Analytics

I use Google Analytics to see which tools are used, how people find them and which pages need work. It records things such as the pages you view, how long you stay, the site that sent you, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or country). Google says Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. I only see totals and trends, not who you are. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

Some pages show ads served by Google AdSense, which helps keep the tools free.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.

Cookies and browser storage

Google Analytics
Cookies named _ga and _ga_… that tell one visit from a repeat visit. They last up to two years.
Google AdSense
Advertising cookies set by Google and its partners on pages that show ads, as described above.
Market data limit
A cookie named yf_req, set only by the DCA simulators, that counts how many times you loaded prices today. It expires at the end of the day.
Remembered work
Local storage holding your last inputs and tool settings. It stays on your device until you clear it.
Offline copies
The service worker's cache of site files, used to load pages quickly and offline.

Services some tools contact

Most pages load fonts from Google Fonts and many load code libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare) or jsDelivr. These services receive your IP address and browser details, as any website you load from does. A few tools also fetch live data, and only when you use that feature:

Load Flow Simulator map
Map tiles from OpenStreetMap, EOX (satellite imagery) and Open Infrastructure Map. Typing in the place search sends that text to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.
Exchange rates
The Cost of Living map and the Rent vs Own sensitivity page can fetch current exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) or the free currency API by fawazahmed0. Only a request for the rates is sent.
DCA simulators
Price history is fetched through a small proxy I run on Cloudflare Workers, which asks Yahoo Finance. It receives the tickers you load and your IP address. To stop abuse it keeps a count of requests per IP address for the day, which is deleted automatically within about 36 hours. It does not keep your tickers or portfolio.

Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The tools keep working without them.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on to stop Analytics on every site.
  • Turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything it has stored on your device.

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Principles or the GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. As I hold no accounts or profiles, most such data sits with Google, GitHub or Cloudflare, but email me and I will help with anything I can reach.

Children

The tools are not aimed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
