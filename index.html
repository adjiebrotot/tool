Random Picker

Pick one option at random with a spinning wheel, a 3D dice roll, a slot machine, or a Galton board.

Active

Type your options, one per line, then pick one at random in whichever way is most fun: spin a wheel, roll a 3D dice, pull a slot machine lever, or drop a ball down a Galton board. The wheel gives every option an equal chance and always picks one. The slot machine and the dice behave like the machines they imitate, so some pulls and rolls come up empty: about 3 pulls in 4 pay out, and the die is an honest 1 in 6 per face. The Galton board is deliberately uneven, so middle options win more often, exactly like a real bean machine. Remove an option once it has been picked to run a draw with no repeats. Everything runs locally in your browser.
