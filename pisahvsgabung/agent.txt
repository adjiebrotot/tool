WHAT THIS TOOL DECIDES
Whether a married couple in Indonesia pays less PPh 21 income tax with Gabung Harta (the husband's
tax return uses PTKP K, and the wife's employer withholds final tax on her salary with her own PTKP)
or with Pisah Harta (PH or MT: the tax is worked out on their combined net income and shared by each
spouse's net income), from each spouse's yearly gross salary, deductions and dependants, and the
household salary at which the cheaper way changes. Requests often come in Indonesian: write the
assumptions in the language the user wrote in.

LINK FORMAT
https://tool.adjiebrotots.com/pisahvsgabung/#load&v=1&qs=<quick start>&<key>=<value>&...&why.<key>=<your reason>&note=<one line>
- After #load come key=value pairs joined by &. Percent-encode each value (a space is %20, & is %26,
  # is %23, + is %2B).
- v=1 is the format version. qs=<key> starts from that Quick Start; without it the tool starts from
  its defaults.
- Then each key in the link is set, in the units its label shows on screen. splitPct=50 means 50%,
  not 0.5. Numbers may carry $, commas and a trailing %. A checkbox takes true or false. A select or
  a choice takes the value listed, or its visible label.
- Anything the link does not name keeps the value it started from, so send only what differs.
- why.<key>=<text>: your reason for that value, shown to the user beside it (200 characters at
  most). note=<text>: one line shown above the list, for example the place and the date.
- A number outside a field's range is pulled into range and the user is told; a key the tool does
  not know is skipped and listed. Never use the keys s, open or embed.

QUICK STARTS (qs)
Three households, each with the statutory PTKP and tax brackets. Start from the closest one and send
the user's own figures. Use double when both spouses earn a salary (it is entered for each spouse),
single when only one earns (a household total, with the wife's share 0), and even for a household
total shared by a percentage (even is the page's own start). A Quick Start sets its own way of
entering income; a link that names the salary fields of the other way switches to it.
qs takes one of: even, single, double. Each opens a worked household with the statutory PTKP and tax
brackets; a link sends only what differs from it.
What each Quick Start sets is listed under QUICK START FIGURES at the end.

FIELDS (key: label on the page)
Most important: inputMode, husbandSalaryInput, wifeSalaryInput, totalSalaryInput, splitPct,
dependents, husbandDeductionInput, wifeDeductionInput, deductionInput.

[Household: Family]
dependents: Dependants (tanggungan)  (most important)
  One of: 0 (No dependants), 1 dependant, 2 dependants, 3 dependants. Default 0.
  Dependants (tanggungan) the household supports, usually children, 0 to 3; more than three count as
  three. Each one adds to the PTKP.
  Tip on the page: Family members you support, such as children. Each one adds to the tax-free
  allowance (PTKP), and at most three count.

[Household: Income]
totalSalaryInput: Household gross salary (both spouses)  (most important)
  Number (Rp before it, /yr after it). 0 to 1,000,000,000,000. Default 500,000,000.
  Both spouses' gross salaries together for a year, in rupiah. Counts when income is entered as
  Total and split (inputMode=total), with splitPct. Use it only when the user gives a household
  total and not each salary.
splitPct: Wife's share of that salary  (most important)
  Slider (%). 0 to 100. Default 50.
  The wife's share of totalSalaryInput, in % as a whole number: 0 means the husband earns it all, 50
  an equal split. Counts only in Total and split.
deductionInput: Deductions, both spouses (pengurang)  (most important)
  Number (Rp before it, /yr after it). 0 to 1,000,000,000,000. Default 0.
  Deductions (pengurang) for both spouses together, in rupiah a year, taken off gross income before
  PTKP and shared by what each earns. Mainly the pension contributions an employee pays (iuran
  pensiun, and the employee's own JHT and JP of BPJS Ketenagakerjaan), never the employer's share.
  Biaya jabatan is separate (biayaJabatan). Leave at 0 unless the user gives them or says they pay
  these, and say so when you assume any. Counts only in Total and split.
  Tip on the page: Deductions such as iuran pensiun, taken off gross income before PTKP and split in
  proportion to what each spouse earns. Biaya jabatan has its own switch; with it off, include biaya
  jabatan here.
husbandSalaryInput: Husband's gross salary  (most important)
  Number (Rp before it, /yr after it). 0 to 1,000,000,000,000. Default 250,000,000.
  The husband's gross salary (gaji kotor suami) for a year, in rupiah, before any tax. A monthly
  salary (gaji sebulan) times 12, plus THR, a 13th month and bonus when the user gives them. Counts

wifeSalaryInput: Wife's gross salary  (most important)

  The wife's gross salary (gaji kotor istri) for a year, in rupiah, worked out the same way. 0 for a
  wife with no salary. Counts when income is entered Each spouse.
husbandDeductionInput: Husband's deductions (pengurang)  (most important)
  Number (Rp before it, /yr after it). 0 to 1,000,000,000,000. Default 0.
  The husband's own deductions (pengurang), as for deductionInput. Counts only in Each spouse.
  Tip on the page: Deductions such as iuran pensiun that come off the husband's gross income only,
  before PTKP. Biaya jabatan has its own switch.
wifeDeductionInput: Wife's deductions (pengurang)  (most important)
  Number (Rp before it, /yr after it). 0 to 1,000,000,000,000. Default 0.
  The wife's own deductions (pengurang), as for deductionInput. Counts only in Each spouse.
  Tip on the page: Deductions such as iuran pensiun that come off the wife's gross income only,

biayaJabatan: Biaya jabatan, worked out for you
  Checkbox: true or false. Default true.
  Biaya jabatan, the allowance for the cost of holding a job: 5% of each spouse's gross salary, at

COMPACT LISTS
- `inputMode=total|individual`
- `brackets=<upTo>:<rate>,<upTo>:<rate>,...,:<rate>`

Items are separated by `,` and their parts by `:`, both typed as they are. Numbers are plain (60000000, 12.5), with no thousands separators.

inputMode: the way income is entered. `total` is Total and split (totalSalaryInput and splitPct, also written "Total dan porsi"); `individual` is Each spouse (husbandSalaryInput and wifeSalaryInput, also "Per pasangan"). A link that names a salary or deduction field of one way switches to that way by itself, so inputMode is only needed to say it outright. The fields of the other way do not count and are listed as not applied. When the way changes, the figures the link does not set are carried over from the other way, as the page's own buttons do. For example:
`inputMode=individual`

brackets: the PPh 21 tax brackets (lapisan tarif), each bracket's upper limit in rupiah a year and its rate in %, from the lowest. The last bracket has no upper limit: write it `:<rate>` (a limit given there is ignored). Limits must rise, at most 5 brackets, rates 0 to 100 in steps of 0.01. It replaces the whole ladder. The tool opens on the statutory ladder (UU HPP, 2022), which is
`brackets=60000000:5,250000000:15,500000000:25,5000000000:30,:35`
The brackets are set by law: leave brackets out unless the user gives different ones, for example to try a proposed change. Never guess them. A ladder of three brackets, 5% up to Rp 60,000,000, 15% up to Rp 250,000,000 and 35% above:
`brackets=60000000:5,250000000:15,:35`
