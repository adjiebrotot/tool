How to use the PPh 21 Pisah vs Gabung

Find which way a married couple's Indonesian income tax costs less, Gabung Harta or Pisah Harta, and by how much a year. Every demo on this page is the real tool, running live in your browser. Press Pause on one to try it yourself.

Quick Start

Three worked couples: Equal incomes, no children (Rp 500 million a year shared equally, the page's starting point), a Single income family (husband Rp 300 million, wife nothing, two children) and a Double income family (husband Rp 400 million, wife Rp 300 million, one child).

On a phone, press Full screen under a demo and turn the phone sideways to read it.

  1. Open the inputs

    On a first visit the card is folded to a rail, so the answer has the room. Click Inputs to open it; it stays the way you leave it.

  2. Pick an example

    Hover a button to read who it is. A click fills the Household tab and the answer follows at once.

  • Three answers. Equal incomes: Gabung Harta saves Rp 18.6M a year. Single income: both ways cost Rp 28.0M, as there is no income of hers to combine. Double income: Gabung Harta saves Rp 33.5M.
  • Biaya jabatan is on in every example. 5% of each salary, at most Rp 6,000,000 a year, comes off before PTKP; no other deduction is entered.
  • No Reset button. Every Quick Start puts PTKP and the brackets back to the statutory values first, so it is the way back to a clean start.

Household

Who depends on you and what each spouse earns, in rupiah a year. Enter income as Total and split (the household salary and the wife's share of it) or Each spouse (each salary and its own deductions).

  1. Dependants

    0 to 3. Each adds Rp 4,500,000 to the PTKP both ways. Two take the saving from Rp 18.6M to Rp 17.6M.

  2. The wife's share

    The slider under Household gross salary; the line under it says what each earns. At 30% the saving falls to Rp 15.0M.

  3. Deductions

    Iuran pensiun and the like, shared by salary. Rp 12,000,000 at 30% puts Rp 8,400,000 on the husband, on top of his biaya jabatan.

  4. Biaya jabatan

    The switch works out 5% of each salary, at most Rp 6,000,000 a year. Off, the deductions carry it instead, and a note flags an amount past the cap, as Rp 8,400,000 is.

  5. Each spouse

    The figures carry across: Rp 350,000,000 and Rp 150,000,000, the deductions split the same way.

  • Gross means before deductions. The salary as the employer pays it, for the whole year.
  • The way back rounds the share. The slider holds whole percents, so Rp 400M and Rp 300M (42.857%) come back from Each spouse as 43%.
  • A deduction is a fixed amount. The charts keep it at the same rupiah figure at every salary. Biaya jabatan is worked out again at each one.
  • A file or link from before the switch opens with it off, so it still gives the answer it gave then.

PTKP & Brackets

The rules, set to the statutory values: the tax-free allowance (PTKP) and the PPh 21 brackets. Change them only to model a different rule.

  1. Tax-free allowance (PTKP)

    Four amounts build every PTKP. The line under them adds them up: Rp 63.0M husband + Rp 54.0M wife under Gabung Harta, Rp 117.0M under Pisah Harta.

  2. Tax brackets

    From (Rp) fills itself in. Edit Up to (Rp) and Rate (%); only the last bracket has no upper limit.

  3. The two ways

    The note under the brackets says how each way is taxed and which PTKP it takes.

  • An edited rule shows. What this assumes then says your own PTKP amounts or brackets apply, not the statutory ones.
  • The ladder stays whole. Raising a limit past the ones beneath it deletes them, and a blank limit is put back.

  • Warnings sit at the foot of the inputs: a share of 0% or 100%, deductions above a salary, and with biaya jabatan off, a deduction past its cap.

Part 2

How the numbers work

How the tool works out PPh 21 for a married couple both ways: every field, where it enters the sums, and what moving it does to the saving.

The model in one picture

The tool taxes the same two salaries twice, once for each way UU PPh Pasal 8 allows, and compares the totals. The headline is Tax savings (cheaper option): the gap between them.

  • Gabung Harta (K/n + TK/0). The husband files one SPT for the family with PTKP K/n. The wife's salary, from one employer and unrelated to his work, is taxed final by her employer with her own PTKP TK/0 (Pasal 8 ayat 1). Two bracket ladders.
  • Pisah Harta, PH or MT (K/I/n). Each spouse files an SPT, but the tax is worked out on the couple's combined net income with PTKP K/I/n (Pasal 8 ayat 3), then shared by each spouse's net income. One ladder.
Saving = TPisah − TGabungabove zero: Gabung Harta is cheaper
TGabung = T( ⌊NH − PK⌋ ) + T( ⌊NW − PTK⌋ )
TPisah = T( ⌊NH + NW − PKI⌋ )
  • NH, NWeach spouse's net income: gross salary less biaya jabatan and deductions
  • PKthe husband's PTKP K/n (Gabung Harta)
  • PTKthe wife's own PTKP TK/0 (Gabung Harta)
  • PKIthe couple's PTKP K/I/n (Pisah Harta)
  • Tthe Pasal 17 brackets, applied to taxable income (PKP)
  • ⌊ ⌋PKP rounded down to whole thousands of rupiah, never below nil

The names run against intuition. Pisah Harta (separate property) is the way that adds the two incomes into one computation. Gabung Harta is the way that taxes the wife's salary on its own ladder. The tool uses the law's names; read each figure by its label, not by the word.

  • Gabung Harta gives each salary its own ladder. Each spouse's first Rp 60M of PKP is taxed at 5% and the next Rp 190M at 15%. Added together, the second salary starts where the first stopped, in the dearer brackets. Once both salaries cover their own PTKP, the statutory allowances add up to the same total both ways, so the ladder is the whole difference.
  • Pisah Harta wins when one spouse earns less than their own PTKP. Under Gabung Harta the unused part of it is lost; combined, it shelters the other spouse's income. Hence the Double income family's crossover at about Rp 126M a year, and Pisah Harta saving Rp 14.6M at Equal incomes with the wife's share at 100%, where the husband's PTKP has nothing of his to shelter.
  • With no income of hers, both ways are the same. The I addition applies only when the wife has net income, so both ways give the husband K/n.
  • The saving has a ceiling. A second ladder saves at most (30% − 5%) × Rp 60M + (30% − 15%) × Rp 190M + (30% − 25%) × Rp 250M = Rp 56M a year. It reaches that once both spouses' PKP passes Rp 500M, and stays there while the combined PKP is under Rp 5B.

  • A rupiah is worth the rate it is taxed at. A deduction or a dependant takes income off the top: under Gabung Harta at that spouse's own rate, under Pisah Harta at the couple's. In the Double income family that is 25% and 30%, so each dependant saves Rp 1,125,000 one way and Rp 1,350,000 the other, and the saving falls by the difference, Rp 225,000.
  • Who earns it matters, not only how much. Pisah Harta taxes the total, so moving salary between spouses leaves it alone. Gabung Harta is cheapest when both PKPs sit in the same bracket: then no rupiah could move to a cheaper bracket on the other ladder.

Biaya jabatan is worked out for you; the rest is yours to enter. The switch takes 5% of each gross salary, at most Rp 6,000,000 a year. The employee's pension contribution (iuran pensiun) goes in the deductions. Turn the switch off to type biaya jabatan yourself: the tool then warns when an amount passes the cap, but applies whatever you type.

PTKP & Brackets: the allowances and the ladder

PTKP is the slice of income that is not taxed at all. What is left, rounded down to whole thousands, is PKP, and the brackets tax it in slices: each rate applies only to the part of PKP inside its bracket.

PK = B + K + n × D, PTK = BGabung Harta: husband K/n, wife TK/0
PKI = B + K + I + n × DPisah Harta: K/I/n; I only when NW > 0
T(x) = Σ rk × ( the part of x between Lk−1 and Lk )
  • Bbase, for each taxpayer: Rp 54,000,000
  • Kadded if married: Rp 4,500,000
  • Iadded when the wife's income is combined: Rp 54,000,000
  • Dadded for each dependant: Rp 4,500,000
  • Lkthe bracket limits: Rp 60M, 250M, 500M, 5B
  • rkthe rates: 5%, 15%, 25%, 30%, 35%

  • The allowances match, by design. I equals B, so the wife brings the same Rp 54M into either way: K/1 + TK/0 is Rp 63M + Rp 54M, and K/I/1 is Rp 117M. Edit one without the other and PTKP starts to pick the winner too.
  • Marginal and average are different rates. The Double income family's last rupiah under Pisah Harta is taxed at 30% (Max marginal bracket: 30% from Rp 500.0M), yet its tax is 16.47% of gross. Crossing into a bracket never raises the tax on income below it.
  • Rounding. PKP is rounded down to whole thousands of rupiah before the rates apply (Pasal 17 ayat 4), separately for each PKP.
  • Who pays under Pisah Harta. The one tax is shared by net income. That changes what each spouse owes, never the couple's total.

Worked example: Double income family

The Quick Start as it loads, worked by hand: husband Rp 400,000,000 a year, wife Rp 300,000,000, one child, biaya jabatan on and no other deductions, statutory PTKP and brackets.

Both ways, from salary to tax

Both salaries are past Rp 120M, where 5% reaches the cap, so each takes the full Rp 6,000,000 of biaya jabatan. Every PKP is already a whole thousand.

  1. Husband's net incomeRp 400,000,000 − Rp 6,000,000 biaya jabatanRp 394,000,000
  2. Husband's PTKP (K/1)Rp 54,000,000 + Rp 4,500,000 + 1 × Rp 4,500,000Rp 63,000,000
  3. Husband's PKPRp 394,000,000 − Rp 63,000,000Rp 331,000,000
  4. Husband's tax5% × Rp 60M + 15% × Rp 190M + 25% × Rp 81M = Rp 3,000,000 + Rp 28,500,000 + Rp 20,250,000Rp 51,750,000
  5. Wife's net incomeRp 300,000,000 − Rp 6,000,000 biaya jabatanRp 294,000,000
  6. Wife's PKP (TK/0)Rp 294,000,000 − Rp 54,000,000Rp 240,000,000
  7. Wife's tax, final5% × Rp 60M + 15% × Rp 180M = Rp 3,000,000 + Rp 27,000,000Rp 30,000,000
  8. Gabung Harta: total taxRp 51,750,000 + Rp 30,000,000; 11.68% of Rp 700M grossRp 81,750,000
  9. Couple's PTKP (K/I/1)Rp 54,000,000 + Rp 4,500,000 + Rp 54,000,000 + Rp 4,500,000Rp 117,000,000
  10. Combined PKPRp 394,000,000 + Rp 294,000,000 − Rp 117,000,000Rp 571,000,000

  • The tool shows the same. Tax savings (cheaper option) Rp 33.5M, a year with Gabung Harta; Gabung Harta: total tax Rp 81.8M, effective rate 11.68%; Pisah Harta (PH/MT): total tax Rp 115.3M, 16.47%. The summary shows deductions Rp 12.0M, PTKP husband Rp 63.0M, wife Rp 54.0M, and K/I/1 Rp 117.0M.
  • Where the Rp 33,550,000 comes from. Stacked on the husband's Rp 331M, the wife's Rp 240M of PKP would be taxed at 25% up to Rp 500M and 30% beyond: Rp 42,250,000 + Rp 21,300,000 = Rp 63,550,000. On her own ladder it is Rp 30,000,000.
  • The split, in rupiah. Rp 115,300,000 × 394 ÷ 688 = Rp 66,029,360 for the husband and × 294 ÷ 688 = Rp 49,270,640 for the wife, shared by net income, as Pisah Harta detail shows at Rp 700M.

What moves it most

Each bar lowers or raises one input by a step worth arguing about, and keeps the rest of the Double income family where it was.

  • Who earns it moves the saving most. Rp 70M more for the husband costs Rp 7.0M of it: that money is taxed at his 25% instead of her 15%. Rp 70M more for the wife adds only Rp 1.00M, as it carries her past Rp 250M into 25% as well.
  • A higher household salary widens the gap, unevenly. 10% less cuts the saving by Rp 6.5M, 10% more adds Rp 4.5M. It stops growing at Rp 56M, once both ladders are full.
  • Dependants and deductions barely touch the choice. They lower both taxes by nearly the same amount: Rp 6M of deductions each takes Rp 2.4M off Gabung Harta and Rp 3.6M off Pisah Harta.
  • For a decision. With both salaries well above their PTKP, Gabung Harta stays cheaper at every step here (the smallest saving is Rp 26,550,000). The answer turns only when one salary falls near or below its own PTKP, around the crossover.

  1. Rp 70M more to the wife

    Husband Rp 330M, wife Rp 370M. The saving edges up to Rp 34.5M; Pisah Harta's tax stays Rp 115.3M.

  2. Rp 70M more to the husband

    Husband Rp 470M, wife Rp 230M. The saving falls to Rp 26.6M, and the charts move with the new split.

  3. Back to the example

    The Quick Start again: Rp 33.5M.

What it leaves out

A comparison of the yearly tax on two salaries under the two computations of Pasal 8, nothing more. What this assumes, under the tool's answer, lists what applies to your household.

  • Salary only. PPh 21 on employment income. Business income, income taxed at a final rate and any other income are left out.
  • No monthly withholding. The tool works out each way's tax for the year. It does not model the TER withholding employers apply each month or the reconciliation in the SPT, so it shows no kurang bayar or lebih bayar. Under Pisah Harta each employer withholds on one salary alone, so the combined computation usually leaves tax to pay when filing.
  • Only biaya jabatan is worked out from gross. Pension contributions and any other deduction are yours to enter, and every salary is taken to be an employee's, entitled to biaya jabatan.
  • Gabung Harta assumes one employer for the wife. Her salary must come from one employer and have nothing to do with her husband's work to be taxed final; otherwise it is added to his.
  • A husband with no income. The tool assumes her employer still uses TK/0. An employer may apply K if she shows he has no income.
  • PH and MT are taxed alike here. Both use the combined computation. Whether you may file PH (it needs a written separation of property and income) is not something the tool checks.
  • The charts hold your split and deductions. Only the household salary moves, with biaya jabatan worked out at each one, from Rp 100 million to Rp 5 billion a year.

When not to lean on it. Income besides salary, a wife with more than one employer or working in her husband's business, or a household near the crossover, where a small change flips the answer. Then ask a tax consultant.
