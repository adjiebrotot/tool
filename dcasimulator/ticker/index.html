Common yfinance Ticker Codes

A reference of common Yahoo Finance ticker codes for Australia, Indonesia, the United States, and Singapore — cash & money market, government & corporate bonds, and major stocks & ETFs.

← DCA Simulator

Yahoo Finance (used by the DCA Scenario Explorer and the Python yfinance library) identifies each security by a ticker code. US listings use a plain symbol; other exchanges add a suffix — Australia .AX, Indonesia .JK, Singapore .SI. The lists below cover the most common cash / money-market, government & corporate bond, and major stock tickers for each market. Type to filter, then copy a code into the simulator's Ticker field.
